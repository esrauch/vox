Lyric timer: We Got Jack Back

Play the song and press Space the moment each line starts being sung: it stamps the line under the orange box and moves on. Press Enter when a line finishes and there's a gap before the next (an instrumental bit), so the screen goes blank there. Backspace undoes the last stamp (and jumps back a few seconds), ←/→ skip 5 seconds, P plays or pauses. Click a line to put the box on it (and jump to just before it, if it's stamped) to redo from there. Your work saves itself in this browser. When it's done, press Download (or copy the text at the bottom) and send it to Claude.
